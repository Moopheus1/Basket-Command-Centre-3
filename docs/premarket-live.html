How the signal is decided (live TradingView price; if you are not connected the cell says so and falls back to the Yahoo snapshot or last close): SELL price is below the support floor (or below the deeper floor); AT SUPPORT price is inside a support range; WAIT above support but under the pivot; BUY price is at or above the pivot; TARGET 1 / 2 HIT price has reached an upside objective that sits above the pivot. “Price vs pivot” is in dollars (green = above, red = below). For a single support level there is no range, so price at or below it reads SELL only once it is under the level. On bearish names the sheet’s “support” is really its bearish target. Each level shows the sheet’s own remark beside it. Where a sheet gives two pivots (AMZN 255.36 / 257.86) or a range (AVGO 391.66 – 409.81), the BUY signal uses the higher number. Upside objectives that sit at or below the pivot are shown but not used for the signal. The sheet says a spike does not count: confirm with a daily or weekly close beyond the level, as its condition says, before acting. These are the sheets’ levels, not advice.

Value, Change and Chg% are your real-time TradingView prices (index futures are the CME contracts ES, NQ, YM and RTY). While this page is connected, TradingView’s window is drawn taller than your screen so the whole watchlist is on screen at once (that is what makes every price update instantly); it goes back to normal as soon as you close this page. Prev is the previous close implied by the change. Open, High and Low are today’s regular-session figures from the Yahoo snapshot, widened by the live price, so High and Low never lag behind the Value. Open and range appear after 9:30am ET; before that they show a dash.

Charts — daily candles, in the same order as the table

Table — default order is A to Z; click a column to sort (click again to reverse), click a ticker to jump to its chart above

Each average cell shows the average in dollars, then how many dollars the price is above (green) or below (red) it. Gap is price minus the last regular close. The Gap column and each average column (EMA9, SMA20, SMA50, SMA100, SMA200) sort by percentage distance from the price (the dollar figure stays in the cell): click once for largest to smallest, click again for smallest to largest.
Pre-market and live prices come from Yahoo Finance 1-minute bars, refreshed about every 5 minutes from 4:00am to 4:55pm ET (GitHub scheduling can run late); Yahoo gives no pre-market volume, and thinly traded names can print odd prices, so treat a single pre-market tick with caution. The averages are computed from the completed daily bars in data.json (EMA9 is exponential; SMA20/50/100/200 are simple), so they do not move with the pre-market price. TradingView charts are TradingView's own embedded widget on daily candles (their data and their orange Pre tag). The free widget cannot hide or recolour lines, so only the SMA 200 is added to those charts (its value shows in the chart legend); all five averages are listed under each chart and in the table. Pick “my SVG” in the chart-type box for charts with all five averages drawn in separate colours. Nothing on this page feeds TAKE, Weekly Picks, any panel or the forward log.
