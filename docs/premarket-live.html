Value, Change and Chg% are your real-time TradingView prices (index futures are the CME contracts ES, NQ, YM and RTY). Prev is the previous close implied by the change. Open, High and Low are today’s regular-session figures from the Yahoo snapshot, widened by the live price, so High and Low never lag behind the Value. Open and range appear after 9:30am ET; before that they show a dash.

Charts — daily candles, in the same order as the table

Table — default order is A to Z; click a column to sort, click a ticker to jump to its chart above

Each average cell shows the average in dollars, then how many dollars the price is above (green) or below (red) it. Gap is price minus the last regular close. The Gap column sorts by percentage so different-priced stocks compare fairly; the sort for each average is also by percentage.
Pre-market and live prices come from Yahoo Finance 1-minute bars, refreshed about every 5 minutes from 4:00am to 4:55pm ET (GitHub scheduling can run late); Yahoo gives no pre-market volume, and thinly traded names can print odd prices, so treat a single pre-market tick with caution. The averages are computed from the completed daily bars in data.json (EMA9 is exponential; SMA20/50/100/200 are simple), so they do not move with the pre-market price. TradingView charts are TradingView's own embedded widget on daily candles (their data and their orange Pre tag). The free widget cannot hide or recolour lines, so only the SMA 200 is added to those charts (its value shows in the chart legend); all five averages are listed under each chart and in the table. Pick “my SVG” in the chart-type box for charts with all five averages drawn in separate colours. Nothing on this page feeds TAKE, Weekly Picks, any panel or the forward log.
